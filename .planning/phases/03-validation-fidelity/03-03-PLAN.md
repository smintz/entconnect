<objective>
Complete D-07's hybrid: protovalidate's own evaluator for every standard rule, a locally compiled
`cel.Env` for custom `(buf.validate.field).cel` rules, one operation-dependent scope computation,
and one shared error constructor — with the two paths' agreement proven by test rather than
assumed.

Purpose: D-07 was a user decision taken against Claude's recommendation, and its recorded cost is
that two independently compiled evaluators must produce identical output. 03-CONTEXT.md is
explicit that this makes the differential proof "the mechanism that keeps this choice safe" and
names the two-paths-one-error-shape convergence as one of the phase's two genuine risk
concentrations. 03-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1 then found the concrete hazard: protovalidate's
`WithFilter` gates at whole-field granularity, not rule-kind granularity, so a field carrying
both a standard rule and a custom CEL rule would have its CEL half evaluated twice by two
engines. Research deliberately left the resolution open; this plan settles it with a test.

Output: `hooks.go` becomes the complete hybrid; `violation.go` gains deterministic ordering and
`(RuleId, FieldPath)` identity; D-06's operation-dependent scope is implemented on whichever
mechanism plan 03-01 empirically proved; and an uncompilable CEL expression fails at schema load
through the existing collected-failure machinery.
</objective>
